Official iOS Installation Guide

Step-by-Step Melora for iPhone & iPad

Enjoy distraction-free music on your iPhone. Apple restricts unsigned App Store downloads, so choose the method that best fits your workflow.

Method 1: Native Safari Web App (PWA)
Takes 15 seconds • No computer required • Never gets revoked by Apple
1
Open the Web Player in Safari
On your iPhone or iPad, open the Safari browser and navigate to the Melora Web Player. (Make sure you use Safari, as iOS only allows Home Screen installations via Safari).
Open Web Player in Safari ↗
2
Tap the Share Icon
At the bottom toolbar of Safari (or top bar on iPad), tap the Share button (the square icon with an upward arrow pointing out: [ ↑ ]).
💡 Look at the bottom center of your iPhone screen
3
Select "Add to Home Screen"
In the iOS Share menu sheet, scroll down past the contact list and tap "Add to Home Screen" (the icon with a plus inside a square ⊞).
4
Confirm and Tap "Add"
A confirmation dialog appears showing the Melora logo and title. Simply tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
5
Launch Melora from your Home Screen
Return to your iPhone Home Screen. You will see the native Melora app icon. Tap it to launch full-screen with no browser address bars, offline caching, and responsive sound.
Zero Expiry Guarantee: Unlike sideloaded apps which expire every 7 days without a paid Apple Developer Account, Safari Web Apps never expire and never get revoked.
Method 2: Sideload via AltStore / Sideloadly
For power users who want the native Flutter engine • Requires a PC or Mac
1
Install AltServer on your PC / Mac
Download and install AltServer from altstore.io. Launch AltServer and enable Wi-Fi sync in iTunes / Finder.
2
Install AltStore on your iPhone
Connect your iPhone via USB. In AltServer on your computer, click Install AltStore > [Your iPhone] and enter your free Apple ID credentials to sign the app.
3
Download the Melora iOS IPA
Download the latest official Melora iOS build file directly from GitHub Releases:
Download Melora.ipa from Releases →
4
Open in AltStore & Sideload
On your iPhone, open AltStore > tap the "+" icon in "My Apps" > select the downloaded Melora.ipa file. AltStore will sign and install it to your home screen.
5
Trust Developer Certificate
On your iPhone, go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Tap your Apple ID and tap "Trust". Melora is now ready to run!
7-Day Sideload Refresh Note: Free Apple IDs require refreshing sideloaded apps every 7 days via AltStore. For a completely hassle-free experience with zero refreshes, we strongly recommend Method 1 (Safari PWA).
Frequently Asked Questions
Why isn't Melora on the official Apple App Store?
Apple's App Store Review Guidelines strictly restrict third-party music apps with open plugin architectures and external audio stream resolvers. Distributing via PWA and Sideloading guarantees complete privacy and 100% ad-free freedom.
Does background playback work on iOS?
Yes! When added to your Home Screen as a Web App, iOS Media Session API integrates with the native iOS Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and Control Center playback controls.
Is the Web Player free of advertisements?
100% yes. Melora has zero commercial tracking, zero ad networks, and will never interrupt your music.
Can I import my Spotify playlists on iPhone?
Yes! Open the Web Player, paste any Spotify playlist URL into the search bar, and Melora will automatically load all tracks instantly.
