Why Melora

Built Different From The Ground Up

No corporate bloat. No telemetry. No paywalls. Just your music, the way it should be.

100% Ad-Free Forever

No ads, no premium tiers, no upsells. Every feature is unlocked from day one. Free as in freedom.

Plugin-Powered Streaming

Extend Melora with community plugins for any music service. YouTube, SoundCloud, or build your own.

Download with Metadata

Save any track with full album art, lyrics, artist info, and proper ID3 tags. Your offline library, perfected.
