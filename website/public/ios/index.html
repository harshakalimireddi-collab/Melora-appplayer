Step-by-Step Melora for iPhone & iPad

Enjoy ad-free streaming on your iPhone with full-screen controls, Lock Screen artwork, and zero revokes. Choose your installation method below:

Method 1: Native Safari Web App (PWA)
Takes 15 seconds • No computer required • Never gets revoked by Apple

Ready to install on this iPhone?

Tap the button to open the Melora Web Player right now in Safari.

🚀 Launch Web Player
1
Open the Web Player in Safari
On your iPhone or iPad, open the Safari browser and navigate to the Melora Web Player. (Make sure you use Safari, as iOS only supports standalone Home Screen installs via Safari).
💡 Tip: If you are already viewing this page on your iPhone, simply tap "Launch Web Player" above!
2
Tap the Share Icon
At the bottom toolbar of Safari (or top right on iPad), tap the Share button (the square icon with an upward arrow pointing out: [ ↑ ]).
📱 Look at the bottom center of your iPhone screen
3
Scroll & Select "Add to Home Screen"
In the iOS Share sheet that slides up, scroll down past the contacts and airplay section and tap "Add to Home Screen" (icon with a plus inside a square ⊞).
4
Confirm and Tap "Add"
A preview window appears displaying the Melora icon and title. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner of your screen.
5
Launch Melora from your Home Screen
Go to your iPhone Home Screen. Tap the new Melora app icon. It launches as a 100% full-screen app with zero Safari URL bars, Lock Screen audio controls, and background streaming!
Zero Expiry Guarantee: Unlike sideloaded IPA apps which expire after 7 days, Safari Web Apps never expire and never need to be re-signed.
Method 2: Sideload via AltStore / Sideloadly
For power users who prefer sideloading the native iOS binary • Requires a PC or Mac
1
Install AltServer on your PC or Mac
Download and install AltServer from altstore.io. Launch AltServer and enable Wi-Fi sync in iTunes / Finder.
2
Install AltStore on your iPhone
Connect your iPhone via USB. In AltServer on your computer, click Install AltStore > [Your iPhone] and enter your free Apple ID credentials to sign the app.
3
Download the Melora iOS IPA Package
Download the latest official Melora iOS build file directly from GitHub Releases:
Download Melora.ipa from Releases →
4
Open in AltStore & Sideload
On your iPhone, open AltStore > tap the "+" icon in "My Apps" > select the downloaded Melora.ipa file. AltStore will sign and install it to your home screen.
5
Trust Developer Certificate
On your iPhone, go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Tap your Apple ID profile and select "Trust". Melora is now ready to run!
7-Day Sideload Refresh Note: Free Apple IDs require refreshing sideloaded apps every 7 days via AltStore. For a completely hassle-free experience with zero refreshes, we strongly recommend Method 1 (Safari Web App).
Frequently Asked Questions
Why isn't Melora on the official Apple App Store?
Apple's App Store Review Guidelines strictly restrict third-party music apps with open plugin architectures and external audio stream resolvers. Distributing via PWA and Sideloading guarantees complete privacy and 100% ad-free freedom.
Does background playback work on iOS?
Yes! When added to your Home Screen as a Web App, the iOS Media Session API integrates with the native iOS Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and Control Center playback controls.
Is the Web Player free of advertisements?
100% yes. Melora has zero commercial tracking, zero ad networks, and will never interrupt your music.
Can I import my Spotify playlists on iPhone?
Yes! Open the Web Player, tap the Spotify icon at the top, paste any public playlist URL, and Melora will load all tracks immediately.
