Pure Audio Power

Built Different from the Ground Up

Melora strips away the corporate bloat, telemetry, and paywalls so you can enjoy your music the way it was meant to be.

100% Ad-Free & Free Forever

Never get interrupted by annoying 30-second audio ads again. Unlimited skips, free song scrubbing, and complete library access.

Hi-Res Lossless Engine

Powered by mpv and native audio decoders. Enjoy lossless bitrates up to 320kbps with full dynamic equalizer controls.

Melora Connect & Sync

Control playback seamlessly across phone, tablet, and PC over local Wi-Fi using zero-configuration mDNS (_melora._tcp).

Extensible Plugin Ecosystem

Custom metadata providers and audio sources using the integrated Hetu script runtime. Add MusicBrainz, ListenBrainz, and more.

Offline Music Vault

Download tracks directly to your device storage with embedded album artwork, synced lyrics, and full ID3 metadata tags.

Zero Tracking & Privacy

No corporate accounts required, no telemetry, and no behavioral tracking. Your listening habits stay strictly on your device.
