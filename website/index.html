Highlights

Engineered for Fidelity

Melora pairs an audiophile audio pipeline with a modern, glass-crafted native interface.

Bit-Perfect Lossless

Direct 24-bit / 96kHz playback with WASAPI and PipeWire hardware output support for studio clarity.

Plugin Architecture

Extend audio sources, lyric providers, and integrations through sandboxed Dart plugin modules.

System Tray Controls

Minimizes quietly to your system tray on Windows and macOS. Seamless background playback with global hotkeys.

Synchronized Lyrics

Millisecond-accurate real-time lyrics with karaoke tracking and automatic multi-provider fallback.

Cross-Device Melora Connect

Control desktop playback directly from your phone on the local network without cloud dependencies.

Private & Ad-Free

Zero tracking, zero analytics telemetry, and zero advertisements. Free software under the MIT License.
